Keep these with the owner

Voids and refunds, price changes, cancelling purchases, approving stock takes, adding users and setting PINs can move money or hide losses. Only the owner can do them, or a cashier with the owner's PIN for voids, refunds and removing cart lines. Voids, refunds and cart removals go in the audit log with who approved them. Price changes are kept in each product's price history, and a cancelled purchase keeps who cancelled it and why.
